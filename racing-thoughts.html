PROJECT 01 / UNREAL ENGINE 5 · C++ · UMG

Racing Thoughts.

Race a friend across a living roller coaster through surreal, dreamlike worlds. The track keeps changing how you drive: ride a rail, slide through a tube, fly across a shaft. Your phone is the steering wheel.

Beauty shots by the Racing Thoughts art team.

TEAM

PRODUCTION
In progress

01

My contribution

I write a large part of the game’s C++: the game loop, the track and its portals, collisions, and the whole interface, from HUD to menus, in UMG. I also wrote our localisation system and editor tools that let our artists dress the track without touching code.

02

Portals with swappable effects

A portal is a thin actor holding a list of effect objects, like Switch Mode, Slow Down or Cinematic, picked from a dropdown in the editor. A new effect is one new class: the portal, the track and the kart stay untouched. Track sections build themselves from the portals, so the two can never get out of sync.

03

Movement in track space

Karts don’t follow the track with physics. They move along the spline by distance, and the current play mode places them on the cross-section. Movement behaves the same at any frame rate, nobody falls off a loop, and passing a portal is a range check that can’t be missed at any speed.

04

UI built for a team

Every widget is a C++ class with a Blueprint on top for the layout, connected through BindWidget. Programmers own the logic, artists own the look, and a missing or renamed element shows up as a compile error instead of a crash.
