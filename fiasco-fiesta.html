PROJECT 04 / UNITY · C# · GAME JAM

Fiasco Fiesta.

A birthday party, a pile of supplies to unpack and a crowd of children to keep happy. What could possibly go wrong? Made in two and a half days at the Xbox Game Camp Belgium game jam.

MY ROLE
Gameplay programmer
TEAM
Team project
PRODUCTION
2.5 days
Get the game on itch.io ↗ View source ↗

01

A game in 2.5 days

With one of those days spent brainstorming, there was no time to write everything from scratch. I took code from my earlier Unity projects, including the lobby and controls from Ghouls’n’Fools, and split it into small scripts that fit together like puzzle pieces. My fellow developer built the truck delivery system.

02

Built so animators don’t wait

I set up the prefabs as ready-made shells, with scripts, colliders and animation hooks in place and clear slots for meshes and animation states. Our animators could drop in their work and see it in game without waiting for me.

03

Looking back

We shipped a fun, playable vertical slice. It could use more onboarding and balancing, but I learned I can keep a team calm and make things work under a tight deadline.
