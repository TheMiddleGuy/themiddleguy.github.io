PROJECT 03 / GRADUATION WORK · UNREAL ENGINE · SONY MOCOPI · CUDA C++

Archery Motion Analysis.

My graduation work: motion sensors and a bit of maths to show archers how steady and consistent their shots really are.

MY ROLE
Developer
TEAM
Solo project
PRODUCTION
In progress

01

Why archery?

I’m a competitive archer, and like many archers, I’ve often trained without a coach watching every shot. This project is the coach’s eye I always wanted.

02

How it works

Sony Mocopi sensors record the archer’s upper body during the shot. Every session is saved and can be replayed on an avatar, shot by shot. Dynamic Time Warping then lines shots up by phase, draw with draw and release with release, so they can be compared even when their timing differs. The heavy number crunching runs in CUDA C++.

03

What’s next

Turning those comparisons into clear stability and consistency scores that archers can follow over time.
